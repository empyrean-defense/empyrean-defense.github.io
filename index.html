About

Empyrean Defense is a modern warfare tower defense game where you build, upgrade, and customize defenses to hold back an enemy invasion. Learn abilities and skills, craft advanced ammunition, and gather resources on the battlefield to strengthen and expand your defense.

Status

v0.15.0 - Highpoint Station, Assassinate Ability, Phase Disruptor & More (September 29, 2026)

Status: 🟢 Live on Steam 🟢 Live on Steam Demo

New Features

  • Added Highpoint Station, an entirely new campaign mission set high in the snowy mountains
  • Added the Tactical Support Jet, a heavily armored enemy aircraft equipped with a forward-mounted rotary cannon
  • Added the Assassinate ability, a Tier II single target attack that instantly eliminates an enemy (excluding high value targets)
  • Added the Bounty Contract skill, which guarantees two larger credit drops from enemies eliminated with the Assassinate ability
  • Added the Silent Blade achievement for eliminating 100 enemies with the Assassinate ability
  • Added the Restoration Protocol skill, which upgrades the Repair ability, restoring 50% more hitpoints to sites and 100% more to landmarks
  • Added the Clear Signal achievement for defending Highpoint Station without any radar installation taking damage
  • Added the Phase Disruptor equipment which increases the armor piercing of all military sites by 8%. Available on the Market for 50,000 tokens or craft at the Foundry using:
    • Heavy Alloy x250
    • Energy Cells x40
    • Void Resonator Core x1
  • Added Ammo estimates on the mission select panel. A new section shows roughly how many rockets, flak shells and sniper rounds a mission needs, for the sites you've unlocked
  • Added Enemy Breaching warnings. A new on-screen warning appears when an enemy is about to reach the end of its path
  • Produces and Drops icons in the Armory. Site tooltips now show what each economy site produces. Enemy tooltips now show what each enemy can drop
  • Market item tooltip update: resources that production sites can make now tell you where, e.g. "Produced at: Fabrication Facility, Refinery (Volatile Compound Synthesis)"

Improvements

  • In the site menu, items that need a site upgrade are grayed out until you buy it. Hover one to see which upgrade it needs. The icons light up as soon as the upgrade is bought
  • Missions can now have more than one landmark. Losing any of them fails the mission
  • Mission flags on the campaign map now show each mission's own landmark icon
  • Market items of the same type and tier are now sorted by price
  • Achievements that can't be earned yet in Early Access now say so in their tooltips
  • Crafting components for the Ballistics Solver and Overcharge Module are now listed in a more consistent order in the Foundry
  • Update the Acid Rain Ability icon
  • Deployment warnings now appear in the full game too, and only warns about abilities when you have one unequipped and an empty slot to put it in

  • Production Site Drop Amounts

      • Engine Components: 2-4 → 4-8

      • Explosives (requires upgrade): 1-4 → 2-4
      • Electronics (requires upgrade): 1-4 → 18-24

  • Scrap Metal: initial market supply raised from 1000 → 1500
  • Engine Components: initial market supply raised from 100 → 300
  • Electronics: initial market supply raised from 300 → 500
  • Significantly increased drop rates of items from High Value Targets
