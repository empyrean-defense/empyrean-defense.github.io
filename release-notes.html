v0.14.0 - Sunflare Dunes (April 30, 2026)

Greetings, Commanders!

A new challenge has arrived. The demo has just been updated with an entirely new mission: Sunflare Dunes. The heat is relentless, and resources are scarce—this one will test your ability to adapt under pressure.

Mission Overview:

Sunflare Dunes takes place on the outskirts of a deserted oil town, where maintaining production is key to your success. This mission introduces a new objective focused on resource generation, requiring you to keep oil flowing while holding back enemy forces.

Tactical Intel:

  • Power the Pumpjacks: Oil production depends on your power grid. Without sufficient power, pumpjacks will stall—prioritize upgrading your solar arrays to keep them running.

  • Keep Them Operational: Pumpjacks are vulnerable to enemy fire. Stay on top of repairs to avoid losing critical production during heavy waves.

  • Balance Defense and Economy: You’ll need to manage both your defenses and your resource generation carefully. Falling behind on oil production can quickly limit your options.

  • Hold the Perimeter: Enemy forces will pressure multiple approaches into the town. Maintain coverage across both entry points to prevent your defenses from being overwhelmed.

v0.13.2 - Frostwind Hold (April 20, 2026)

Greetings, Commanders!

A new challenge has arrived. The demo has just been updated with an entirely new mission: Frostwind Hold. Pack your cold weather gear—this one is going to be a struggle for survival.

Mission Overview:

Frostwind Hold introduces a frozen mountain pass centered around a remote power utility base. This mission offers a faster pace and tactical challenge compared to the previous two demo missions. Your objective is to secure the region and prevent General Zharos from seizing control of the power infrastructure.

Tactical Intel:

  • Restore the Grid: You’ll need to prioritize bringing the Power Plant online. Doing so restores power to the deployed Tesla Coil—your best asset for holding the front line of incoming enemy waves.
  • Airborne Threats: There are no existing anti-air defenses at the base. Prioritize building these yourself as soon as the power is back online.
  • Watch the Flanks: Be sure to maintain coverage on the mountain pass that runs alongside the hold to prevent enemies from bypassing your main defenses.
