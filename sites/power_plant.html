Power Plant

A rugged, high capacity energy site designed for sustained operation in demanding environments. Its reinforced infrastructure and fuel driven turbines allow it to deliver massive amounts of power to support extensive defensive systems. Built for endurance rather than elegance, it represents the workhorse of your energy network, reliable, powerful, and built to keep the lights on.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 8000 hp
  • Cost: 800
  • Build Time: 4s
  • Power Produced: 600
