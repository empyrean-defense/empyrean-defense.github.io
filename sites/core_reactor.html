Core Reactor

Next generation power site built around six high efficiency plasma conduits, each stabilized by cooled chambers and synchronized energy regulators. Emitting a distinct blue glow from its upper ducts, the facility represents a pinnacle in military energy infrastructure. Designed for operations requiring massive sustained output, the Core Reactor is typically reserved for only the most critical installations or experimental technologies hidden from the public eye.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 12000 hp
  • Cost: 3000
  • Build Time: 6s
  • Power Produced: 2000
