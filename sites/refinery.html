Refinery

Converts crude oil into refined barrels and produces propellant, both used in crafting specialized ammunition. With upgrades, it operates more efficiently and gains the capability to synthesize volatile compounds necessary for high powered munitions. A reliable source of fuel and propellant for your production pipeline.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 4000 hp
  • Cost: 800
  • Build Time: 2s
  • Power Consumed: 400
  • Production: Oil Barrel (1), Propellant (4 - 8)
  • Production Time: 30s

Upgrades

Icon Name Description Cost
Reinforced Structure Boosts maximum hitpoints by an amount equal to 50% base hitpoints and increases armor rating by +10% 400
Armor Plating Boosts maximum hitpoints by an amount equal to the base hitpoints and increases armor rating by +25% 800
Auxiliary Processing Stack Equips the refinery with an auxiliary processing stack, increasing production speed by 25% 1000
