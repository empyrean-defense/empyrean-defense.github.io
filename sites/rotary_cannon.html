Rotary Cannon

Automated defense turret built for continuous suppressive fire. Its multi barrel system delivers a relentless stream of rounds, making it highly effective against fast or lightly armored targets. Though it lacks heavy impact, its unmatched rate of fire makes it a dependable first line of defense against advancing ground forces.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 10000 hp
  • Damage: 50
  • Range: 50m
  • Rate of Fire: 6
  • Crit Chance: 5%
  • Armor Piercing: 15%
  • Cost: 2000
  • Build Time: 4s
  • Power Consumed: 200
