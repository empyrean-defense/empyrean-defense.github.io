Mining Camp

A key resource site established to extract valuable gold and crystals from the terrain. These materials are crucial to the realm’s broader war effort, making their collection a top priority during operations. Securing enough harvested resources is essential for mission success and long term stability across the realm.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 10000 hp
  • Cost: 300
  • Build Time: 2s
  • Power Consumed: N/A
  • Production: +15 Gold/Crystals
  • Production Time: 6s
  • Crystal Chance: 10%
