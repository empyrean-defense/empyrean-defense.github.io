Ion Cannon

Satellite linked weapon platform capable of unleashing a focused ion beam from orbit. After a lengthy charge period, it fires a colossal blast that vaporizes anything caught in its impact zone. Its destructive potential is unrivaled, capable of destroying the strongest enemies.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 15000 hp
  • Damage: 1000
  • Range: 80m
  • Rate of Fire: 0.2
  • Crit Chance: 0%
  • Armor Piercing: 100%
  • Cost: 4000
  • Build Time: 6s
  • Power Consumed: 1000
