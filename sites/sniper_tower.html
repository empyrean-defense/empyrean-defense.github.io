Sniper Tower

Specializes in precision strikes from a distance, targeting high priority enemies before they reach your defenses. Each shot delivers devastating impact, capable of eliminating light and mid tier threats with surgical accuracy. Its slow rate of fire is offset by exceptional range and reliability, making it an indispensable site for commanders who value precision and control over brute force.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 3000 hp
  • Damage: 250
  • Range: 90m
  • Rate of Fire: 0.4
  • Crit Chance: 40%
  • Armor Piercing: 10%
  • Cost: 1200
  • Build Time: 2s
  • Power Consumed: N/A
