Field Tent

A modest research outpost, enabling basic upgrades and enhancements for military sites. Though temporary in structure and limited in scope, it provides the groundwork for early technological progress. Every great defense begins here, with simple tools, improvised setups, and the first steps toward advanced warfare.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 3000 hp
  • Cost: 800
  • Build Time: 2s
  • Power Consumed: 200
