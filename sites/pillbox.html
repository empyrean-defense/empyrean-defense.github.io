Pillbox

Compact, fortified structure designed for early stage defense. Equipped with a rapid fire machine gun, it excels at cutting down infantry and lightly armored units before they can reach your front line. Though limited in range and power, its durability and low cost make it the cornerstone of any early defensive setup.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 5000 hp
  • Damage: 2
  • Range: 40m
  • Rate of Fire: 4
  • Crit Chance: 3%
  • Armor Piercing: 5%
  • Cost: 800
  • Power Consumed: N/A
