Financial Hub

Serves as the economic nerve center of the region, leveraging government contracts, military supply deals, and high level logistics coordination to generate a steady stream of operational funding. Heavily secured and staffed with analysts and procurement officers, this facility ensures a consistent passive income of credits to support frontline efforts, site construction, and sustained resource deployment across the realm.
