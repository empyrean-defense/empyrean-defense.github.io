Missile Launcher

Fortified defense system built for precision strikes. Its guided munitions lock onto both ground, airborne, and naval targets, delivering explosive impacts that can level heavily armored threats. While its reload cycle limits sustained fire, each missile packs devastating power, making this site a cornerstone of any advanced defensive grid.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 10000 hp
  • Damage: 250
  • Range: 75m
  • Rate of Fire: 1
  • Crit Chance: 15%
  • Armor Piercing: 35%
  • Cost: 2400
  • Build Time: 4s
  • Power Consumed: 400
