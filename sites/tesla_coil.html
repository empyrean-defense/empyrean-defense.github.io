Tesla Coil

Channels concentrated electrical energy into precise, high voltage discharges. Each strike releases a surge powerful enough to melt through armor and halt advancing units in their tracks. Though it targets one enemy at a time, its raw stopping power makes it a valuable defense against tougher, high priority threats.

Stats

  • Hitpoints: 10000 hp
  • Damage: 50
  • Range: 50m
  • Rate of Fire: 10
  • Crit Chance: 0%
  • Armor Piercing: 70%
  • Cost: 2800
  • Build Time: 4s
  • Power Consumed: 1000
